THE LETTER

to kurt,

i honestly don't know where to start,,, kasi ang dami kong gustong sabihin, and i don't think one letter would be enough to put everything into words. pero i'll try, kahit alam kong baka may mga thoughts akong hindi maayos na ma-explain. bear with me muna, okay? HAHAHAHAHA.

first of all, i want you to know how i see you. you're someone who unexpectedly became one of the reasons why my ordinary days feel a little better. someone i didn't really expect to become this important to me, pero here we are. it's funny kasi hindi naman kita hinanap, hindi naman kita pinagplanuhang gustuhin, and i definitely didn't expect na aabot ako sa point na magsusulat ako ng ganito tungkol sa'yo. pero somehow, you became someone i look forward to seeing and talking to. someone na kapag may nangyari sa araw ko, kahit gaano ka-insignificant, minsan naiisip kong ikwento sa'yo.

and i think that's one of the things that made me realize na something was changing. kasi dati, i could go through my day without thinking much about whether i'd get to talk to you or not. ngayon, somehow, i find myself looking forward to those little interactions. kahit simpleng conversation lang, kahit asaran lang, kahit correcting my grammar for the nth time, it makes my day a little better. nakakainis, actually. HAHAHAHAHA.

there are so many things i appreciate about you, kurt. and i don't think you realize how much i notice them.

i appreciate how you tiis my kakulitan. how you keep telling me na hindi ka uncomfortable kahit minsan parang ako na mismo yung nagdududa kung comfortable ka pa ba talaga. i appreciate how you try to keep up with my wrong and aching grammar, how you correct me whenever i say something incorrectly, and how you entertain my attempts at using complicated english words kahit minsan halatang pinag-isipan ko pa nang sampung minuto yung sentence ko. i appreciate how you allow me to be noisy, makulit, and completely random around you. hindi mo ako pinaparamdam na kailangan kong maging tahimik or baguhin yung personality ko just because you're naturally the quieter one.

i like how i can be unapologetically makulit around you. how i can suddenly bring up the most unrelated topic, ask the most unnecessary question, or say something that probably makes you wonder kung ano na naman ang tumatakbo sa utak ko. i like that i don't always have to think twice before being myself around you. and maybe you don't think much of it, pero malaking bagay siya sa akin.

i appreciate how you come with me to the library kahit you could've spent that time with your friends, rested, or slept instead. you could've chosen to spend those moments somewhere else, pero you still accompany me. and i don't know if you understand how much i appreciate that. sometimes, it's not even about what we're doing. it's just the fact na you're there. na i get to spend a little time with you, even if we're not necessarily doing anything special.

and PLEASE, god knows how much i hate being called by my first name, george. HAHAHAHAHA. like, i genuinely don't like hearing it, pero pag ikaw,,, letche. suddenly, i don't mind it as much. it's such a stupid little detail, but it's true. imagine having a name that i usually hate being called by, tapos ikaw lang pala yung exception. how unfair of you, honestly.

i also appreciate how you let me drink from your drink or eat your food kahit minsan pilit pa. how you tolerate my little requests, my random questions, and my habit of bothering you over things that could probably wait. i remember these things, kurt. maybe not every single detail, but enough to make me realize na i've been paying attention to you more than i intended to.

and what's funny is that we're so different. you're so quiet, so composed, so nonchalant. you've apparently been like that since g7, and then there's me, who seems to have an unlimited supply of unnecessary words. you're good at english, while i'm still fighting for my life trying to construct a grammatically acceptable sentence. sometimes i genuinely feel like i need to study an entire dictionary just to keep up with you.

you have no idea how many times i've reconsidered the way i phrase things because i don't want my sentence to sound ridiculous. how many times i've searched for a word just because the one i originally thought of doesn't quite express what i mean. and sometimes, i get excited when i learn a new word because i think, "oh, i can use this next time we talk." HAHAHAHAHA. imagine being this academically challenged just because of one person.

but i hope you know na hindi ko naman ginagawa 'yon just to impress you or prove that i can match your vocabulary. i know we're different, and i'm not trying to become a version of myself that sounds more like you. i just want to be able to express myself better, especially when i'm talking to you. kasi ang frustrating minsan when i have so much to say pero hindi ko mahanap yung exact words to explain it.

sometimes, i genuinely wonder, "bakit si kurt?" out of all people, bakit ikaw? bakit someone so quiet and seemingly impossible to reach? bakit someone na hindi ko naman originally expected na magiging ganito ka-important sa akin? i keep asking myself what exactly made me like you, and i think the answer is that there isn't just one thing. it's everything, even the little things you probably don't remember doing.

i don't know if you're just naturally kind. i don't know if you're simply being a decent person, if you treat everyone this way, or if i'm just romanticizing moments that are completely ordinary to you. maybe i'm assigning meanings to things that were never meant to mean anything. maybe you're just being yourself, and i'm here making a whole story out of every little interaction.

and i don't want to assume anything, really. i don't want to mistake your kindness for something else, and i don't want to make you feel like every little thing you do has to mean something more to me. you don't owe me anything just because i happen to appreciate you this much.

but regardless of what those moments mean to you, i know what they mean to me. and i think that's enough reason for me to finally admit this.

i like you, kurt. and nakakainis kasi i really, REALLY didn't want this to happen. i told myself na i wouldn't let myself develop a crush on a classmate. ayoko ng ganitong situation where i have to see someone almost every day and pretend na wala lang, when in reality, i find myself noticing the smallest things about them. i didn't want to complicate a friendship, especially one that i genuinely enjoy having.

pero ikaw kasi, epal ka masyado.

i didn't plan this. hindi naman ako nagising one day na suddenly decided na gusto na kita. it happened gradually. somewhere between your corrections, our conversations, your patience, the times you accompanied me, the random things we talked about, and the moments when you simply let me be myself. i think i just got used to having you around, until one day, i realized na hindi na pala basta-basta yung happiness na nararamdaman ko whenever you're there.

and maybe that's what scares me. because i don't want to lose something that has become important to me. i don't want one confession to turn our usual conversations into something awkward, or make you feel like you have to be careful around me. i still want to be able to bother you, ask you random questions, laugh at stupid things, and have our little vocabulary battles without constantly thinking about this letter.

i hope you understand that i'm not telling you all of this because i'm expecting something in return. i'm not asking you to like me back, and i'm not asking you to give me an answer just because i've finally gathered the courage to say what i feel. i just don't want to keep pretending na wala lang lahat. i don't want to keep dismissing my feelings every time i catch myself appreciating you a little too much.

if you don't feel the same way, that's okay. really. i'd be lying if i said it wouldn't sting a little, but i would never want you to force yourself into feeling something that isn't there. i'd rather have your honesty than a response you only give because you don't want to disappoint me.

and if ever,,, i hope whatever you decide won't make our friendship awkward. i hope we can still be the same people around each other. i hope you can still correct my grammar without thinking na may hidden meaning behind every correction. i hope i can still be makulit without you feeling obligated to entertain me. and i hope you won't suddenly feel like you have to treat me differently just because you know.

i know things might feel a little different after this, and i'm willing to accept that possibility. i just hope we don't immediately throw away something we've already built because of one honest confession.

i'd rather take the risk than keep wondering. i'd rather say it now than spend more time thinking about all the things i could've said but didn't. at least this time, i know i tried. at least this time, i didn't let my fear decide everything for me.

so, kurt, thank you. thank you for being someone who unexpectedly made my days better. thank you for your patience, for entertaining my randomness, for correcting me, for accompanying me, for letting me be noisy, and for all those little things that you probably didn't even realize i was keeping in mind.

you might just be doing ordinary things, but somehow, you made them memorable to me.

and i hope you know that you don't have to do anything extraordinary to be appreciated. sometimes, just being you has already been enough.

i like you. more than i expected, more than i planned, and probably more than i know how to properly explain.

also, please don't correct my grammar after reading this. i've already sacrificed enough of my remaining brain cells trying to write this letter. let me have this one moment. 😭

— g.
